❄️ Snow LandLAST ONE STANDING WINS

Take turns in a snowy field. On your turn, walk up to 2 squares, then do one thing:

  • ✋ Scoop up 1 snow
  • ❄️ Throw a snowball (1 snow) in a straight line, 4 squares
  • 🧱 Wall (3 snow) blocks snowballs. Two hits break it.
  • 🗼 Tower (5 snow) under your feet. Throw 7 squares from up there, and only someone on another tower within 4 squares can hit you!

Get hit and you're out. Trees stop snowballs too.
